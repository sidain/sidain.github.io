Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This application is a personal tool operated for individual use. It accesses Gmail data strictly on behalf of the authorized account owner to read, filter, and modify messages according to the owner's scripts.

Data Handling

  • No third-party sharing: Email contents and metadata are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • No advertising: Gmail data is never used for advertising or profiling.
  • Storage & retention: OAuth credentials and any processed data remain under the account owner's control and can be revoked at any time via Google Account Permissions.
